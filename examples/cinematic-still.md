# 静帧示例：停电后的阅览室

用户：写一个横幅提示词，一位年长管理员在停电后的阅览室整理借阅卡，不生成图片。

决策：单张、16:9；手与卡片是第一眼信息，空书架支持地点，暗部表达停电。不添加额外动作层或组图身份锁。

```text
A quiet library reading room during a power outage. Exactly one older librarian with fine facial lines and weathered hands sits at a wooden desk, sorting a small stack of borrowing cards into a shallow tray. Medium-wide framing from desk height; the hands and cards are the primary readable unit, a few shelves establish the room, and the unlit aisle remains a quiet low-detail area. Cool daylight from a high window falls across the desk, revealing worn wood and the matte paper edges while leaving the far shelves dim. The card backs face the viewer with no readable text. Preserve the single seated person and the clear separation between the hands, cards and tray. Aspect ratio: 16:9.
```

检查点：只有一人；手和卡片接触可信；光源来自窗户。以上是文本示例，不是已生成图片的验收记录。
